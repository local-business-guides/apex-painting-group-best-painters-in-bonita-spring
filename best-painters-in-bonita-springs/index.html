Key facts about scope, timing and cost

A painting project generally involves agreeing on the surfaces to be painted, preparing them, applying paint and allowing appropriate drying time. The exact sequence and materials depend on the job.

  • What it covers: Apex Painting Group handles interior and exterior painting for homes and businesses.

Comparing painters in Bonita Springs

To compare the best painters in Bonita Springs for your needs, request a written description of the work and check that each provider is addressing the same rooms or exterior surfaces. Ask how they assess existing paint, what preparation they expect to do and how they’ll communicate if the condition of a surface changes the plan.

Notice whether the answers are specific to your project. A useful conversation should make it easier to understand the proposed work, not leave you guessing about what a phrase in the proposal means.

A few terms you may hear: prep means getting a surface ready for paint; coat is one application of paint; scope is the agreed list of work. Drying time is the period paint needs before the next step or normal use, as applicable.
