Scope, timing, cost, and useful terms

For this type of request, clarify the project before scheduling. A typical discussion may cover:

  • What it covers: the specific wall or room, surface preparation, paint application, and cleanup. Confirm whether artwork, a pattern, or only a solid-color finish is included.
  • Typical timing: the schedule depends on the size and condition of the area, preparation needs, number of colors, and level of detail. Ask the provider for a project-specific estimate of the work time.
  • Cost factors: the area to be painted, surface repairs, materials, number of colors, and detailed artwork can affect the overall cost. Request a written scope and quote rather than relying on a general figure.
  • Terms you may hear: “surface preparation” means getting the wall ready for paint; “primer” is a preparatory coating; “finish” describes the final look, such as matte or glossy.

When comparing providers, ask to see relevant examples of similar work and confirm who will create any image or design. Check that the written description names the surfaces, preparation, number of coats if specified, and any artistic details.
