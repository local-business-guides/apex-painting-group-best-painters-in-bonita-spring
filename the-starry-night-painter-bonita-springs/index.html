What to clarify, from prep to finish

A clear request makes it easier to discuss the work and get a useful quote. Cover these points:

  • Included work: name each wall or surface, and state whether the request is ordinary painting, a decorative pattern, or a detailed image.
  • Preparation: ask what will happen before paint goes on. “Prep” is short for surface preparation, which can include getting the surface ready for coating; the exact work depends on its condition.
  • Timing: the expected schedule depends on the area, preparation, number of colors, and detail. A provider should discuss timing for your specific scope rather than offer a blanket assumption.
  • Cost considerations: project size, repairs, paint and other materials, and decorative detail can all affect cost. Ask for a quote that matches the written scope; no general price can establish what your particular job will cost.
  • Finish and coats: “finish” means the appearance of the painted surface, while a “coat” is one application of paint. Confirm the finish you want and what the provider plans to include.

A useful comparison also checks whether the provider has experience with the kind of result you’re requesting. Ask for examples of similar work, confirm who is responsible for design choices, and make sure the agreed description distinguishes painting from any artwork or design work.
